CLARIAH-NL is the Dutch contribution to the European Infrastructures CLARIN and DARIAH. Within the Netherlands, CLARIN-NL and DARIAH-NL have joined forces in CLARIAH-NL.

CLARIAH-NL (Common Lab Research Infrastructure for the Arts and Humanities) provides researchers with access to large collections of digital data and to innovative and user-friendly applications for the processing of these data. Both the data and applications are managed in a sustainable manner so that they can also be useful in the future for researchers: from literary researchers, historians and archaeologists to linguists, speech technologists and media scientists.
