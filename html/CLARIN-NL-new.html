Welcome to CLARIN-NL

CLARIN-NL is the Dutch infrastructure for language resources and technologies. It is the Dutch part of the European infrastructure CLARIN-ERIC and has played a key role in this infrastructure right from the start.

The Common Language Resources and Technology Infrastructure (CLARIN) aims to make existing and future language resources easily accessible for researchers and to bring eScience to humanities disciplines. Any researcher can, through federated authentication (single sign-on), gain access to a vast body of language resources, including e.g. speech recordings, literary and historic archives, linguistic corpora, etc. See the CLARIN Virtual Language Observatory and the CLARIN Resource Families for full details.

In addition, CLARIN offers services for depositing, managing, citing, searching and processing language data.

CLARIN has great societal benefits. It transcends disciplines, languages and borders and puts the languages and cultures of a country on the map. By relying on shared central services and adhering to common standards, participating countries and institutions also achieve substantial cost savings through collaboration and reduced duplication of effort.

Within the Netherlands, CLARIN-NL and DARIAH-NL (the Dutch counterpart of the Digital Research Infrastructure for the Arts and Humanities DARIAH-ERIC) have joined forces in CLARIAH-NL, the Common Lab Research Infrastructure for the Arts and Humanities.
